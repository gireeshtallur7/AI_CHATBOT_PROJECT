what are models ?
models are core intelligence layer
they generate text,embeddings or other outputs
they connects our applications to LLM providers like openai,claude,anthropicAI

what is LLMs?
LLMs are large language models 
where the models are trained or learned on large datasets
such as wikipedia,books,articles,code,websites etc in many different language
such worldwide languages also with programming languages like python
and models are deep learning systems (a neaural networks) that learns 
patterns from that large datasets 

LLM will does not search google or think like human it just converts the 
words to numbers called embeddings or vectors looks at the patterns it learned or 
trained predict the next most likely word and keeps predicting 
word by word until to get the required result 

promt is the instruction that we give to the language model 
it tells what to do,
how to respond,
what format to use 
without these promts models does not have direction

LLMs are powerfull but they are not mind readers.
the quality of output depends on the promt that we are gives to the model
better promt --> better result or output
